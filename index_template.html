DJI RC2 Waypoint File Injection Guide

System Compatibility: This tool compiles missions into the modern DJI WPML V2 format.
  • Supported Drones: DJI Mini 4 Pro, DJI Air 3, Mavic 3 Series (Classic/Pro/Enterprise), Inspire 3, Matrice 30/300/350, and other newer aircraft using the DJI Fly App or DJI Pilot 2.
  • Unsupported Drones: Older DJI models (e.g., DJI Mini 3 Pro, Mavic Air 2, Mavic 2, Phantom 4, etc.) which do not support WPML V2 waypoint files.

Since the consumer DJI Fly App does not support direct KML/KMZ waypoint imports from its user interface, you must use a directory overwrite workaround to load this custom flight mission:

Using a Split Mission? If your mission exceeded the flight time limit and downloaded as a .zip file, unzip the folder first on your computer or phone to extract the individual part files (e.g., Part1.kmz, Part2.kmz). Each part must be injected as its own separate waypoint folder.
  1. Create placeholder mission(s): On your DJI RC2 controller, open the DJI Fly App, select Waypoint Flight, and create a short dummy mission (e.g., place 2 arbitrary waypoints) for each part of your flight. Name them memorably (e.g., Placeholder Part 1, Placeholder Part 2) and Save them.
  2. Connect your RC2 to your PC or Android Phone: Use a data-capable USB-C cable to connect your RC2 controller to your computer or an Android phone (like a Samsung Galaxy with MTP support).
  3. Locate the Waypoints Directory: In your computer's file explorer or your phone's file manager app, navigate to the controller's internal storage: 
    DJI RC 2/Internal Shared Storage/Android/data/dji.go.v5/files/waypoint/
    Note: On Android phones, you can use a file manager app (like Samsung "My Files" or "Files by Google") to browse the RC2 storage. On macOS, you will need to use OpenMTP or a similar utility.
  4. Identify your dummy missions: Inside that directory, you will see folders with random names. Look for the most recently updated folders containing a .kmz file.
  5. Inject the generated files:
    • For a single mission: Rename the downloaded `.kmz` file to match the exact filename of the dummy `.kmz` (e.g., Waypoint_XXX.kmz), and copy it into that folder, overwriting the original.
    • For a split mission: Take each extracted part KMZ file (e.g., Part1.kmz) and overwrite the dummy KMZ in its respective placeholder folder.
  6. Fly the mission: Unplug the controller, open the DJI Fly App, go to your saved waypoint missions, and load the placeholders. You will see your newly generated grid patterns ready on the screen!
Safety Warning: Always verify your path on the RC2 screen prior to takeoff. Make sure the relative waypoint heights do not intersect with any trees, buildings, or powerlines in your actual flight environment. Maintain visual line of sight during automated flights!

About Aalaapi Sky

Aalaapi Sky

"Look in a particular direction, observe" (Myaamia / Miami-Illinois)
Version 1.31.0

A waypoint mission generator and editor for drone flight planning. Designed for high-fidelity 2D mapping and 3D Gaussian Splats.

Key Capabilities:

  • Import existing KMZ files to view, drag, and modify waypoints.
  • Edit individual waypoint height, pitch angle, and custom yaw values.
  • Preserve imported filename and structure on KMZ exports.
  • Procedural flight generation: 2D Nadir Grid, 3D Double Grid, Oblique Orbits, and Multi-Tier Hybrid passes.
