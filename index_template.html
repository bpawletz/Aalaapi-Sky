Aalaapi Sky Pilot Guide & Workflow

Plan, validate, and export survey-grade drone waypoint missions for DJI Fly and multi-vendor autopilots in 4 steps:

Step 1 📍
Locate & Select Patterns

Use the topbar search or GPS quick-locate to center the map. Pick a flight tool in Section 1: 2D Nadir Grid, Double Grid (3D Splats), Orbit, Road Follow, Freeform, or 3D Exclusion Zones.

Step 2 🎯
Inspector & Failsafes

Use Tab 1 (Layer Properties) to set layer-specific altitude, speed, front/side overlap, and gimbal pitch (-60° for Gaussian Splats). Use Tab 2 (Mission Failsafes) for global Return-to-Home (RTH), loss-of-signal actions, and target drone model.

Step 3 ⚡
Live Telemetry & Weather

Check the Topbar Telemetry HUD Pill for real-time waypoint count, distance, line/photo spacing, and estimated flight duration. Tap the pill to view live NOAA weather ceiling and visibility checklists.

Step 4 🚀
Auto-Plan, Replay & Export

Use Auto-Plan to bound structures, preview flight trajectories in the 3D Viewer, run pre-flight diagnostics, and download DJI-compliant KMZ or sync directly over USB to your connected DJI RC 2 controller.

📸 Lateral Frustum Culling & Camera Aspect Ratio Selector (4:3 Photo vs 16:9 Video)

Prevents drones from flying parallel flight legs where the target is completely out of frame. Modulates lateral across-track camera footprint with framing sweet-spot ratios, pruning outer lines that only glance the target at extreme sensor corners. Introduces an explicit Camera Aspect Ratio control (4:3 Photo Standard vs 16:9 Video Crop) with dynamic HFOV/VFOV calculation and live badge feedback.

✂️ Target Splat Auto-Pruning of Distant Lead-Up Photos & Live Optical Diagram

Eliminates excessive horizon lead-up and unhelpful distant photos when flying with oblique gimbal angles (e.g. -45°). Uses Photogrammetric Effective Framing to constrain upper field-of-view flare, cutting out up to 50% of wasted shots in neighboring yards. Features an interactive Framing & Lead-Up selector (Balanced Sweet Spot, Tight Framing, or Full Frustum) and a live Optical Standoff & Lead-Up SVG diagram showing side-profile drone elevation, boresight ray, and dynamic lead-in distance savings.

📐 Target Splat Auto-Determined Grid Dimensions & Auto-Fit Toggle

Eliminates cut-off facades and oversized flight areas. The geometry engine dynamically calculates optimal Survey Width and Height directly from your Target Radius or Freeform Polygon, flight altitude, gimbal pitch, and framing margins. Retains full manual control: dragging either slider switches to manual mode, and tapping ⚡ Auto-Fit Grid instantly snaps dimensions back to optimal geometry.

🛫 Flight Leg Phase Indicators & Hover Breadcrumb Ribbons

Provides complete visual clarity on when every waypoint parameter takes effect. Features color-coded badges (PRE ↗ approach leg, AT 📍 stationary coordinate action, POST ↘ departure leg) across 2D map popups and the 3D FPV HUD. Hovering any badge reveals an interactive 3-stage breadcrumb ribbon ([ Approach Leg ] ──▶ [ WP N ] ──▶ [ Departure Leg ]). Automatically detects flight boundary conditions: Waypoint 0 adapts Heading to on-axis takeoff rotation, while the final waypoint marks Flight Speed and Turn Mode as N/A ⛔ with dimmed controls and route-end explanations.

Establishes a unified 3-tier cascade: Global Defaults → Layer Properties → Waypoint Overrides. Each layer can independently configure Capture Mode (Stop & Shoot, Continuous, Video), Connection Path Type (Curved, Straight), Heading Mode, and Target POI with 🌐 Inherit Global fallbacks. Features universal auto-settling delays across all flight plans (3s tilt, 4s/5s turns) to prevent RC 2 gimbal errors, plus Turnaround Overshoot buffers and high-speed transit between survey lines.

Define Box or Freeform Polygon no-fly volumes with configurable 3D altitude bounds (floor/ceiling or all altitudes). The routing engine automatically prunes encroaching waypoints and synthesizes shortest-path perimeter detours.

DJI RC 2 Sync & Transfer Guide

⚡ Bridge Service (Port 8765): The Companion Bridge is a zero-dependency local Node.js background service (tools/companion/server.js) that enables direct 1-click mission synchronization and flight log replay over USB MTP.
  1. Start the Companion Service: Double-click start-companion.bat in the repository root, or run the following command in your terminal:
    npm run companion
  2. Verify Bridge Status: Once started, the sidebar companion box will illuminate `🟢 Bridge Service: Online (port 8765)`.
  3. Prerequisites & Troubleshooting:
    • Ensure Node.js (v18 or higher) is installed on your computer.
    • If port 8765 is occupied or the service fails to start, run npm run companion:stop or terminate lingering Node processes.
    • Remote LAN Access (Tablets & Phones): The companion service binds to all network interfaces (0.0.0.0). Look at your terminal startup log for your Wi-Fi IP (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8765) and open that URL directly in your tablet's browser, or click the ⚙️ icon beside Bridge Service in the sidebar to configure the host IP.
    • Browser Private Network Access: If your browser prompts for local network permissions, select "Allow".
Next Step: Once the service is online, switch to the RC 2 USB Link tab to connect your remote controller.
🔌 RC 2 USB-C & MTP Setup: Connect your physical DJI RC 2 controller to your PC using a certified data-capable USB-C cable to transfer missions and telemetry.
  1. Use a Certified Data Cable: Use a high-quality USB-C cable that supports data transfer. Avoid cheap "charge-only" cables which lack data pins and cannot communicate with Windows.
  2. Power On the Controller: Press once, then press and hold the power button on your DJI RC 2 to turn it on completely before connecting.
  3. Select "File Transfer (MTP)" on the Screen: When plugged into your computer, a prompt will appear on the RC 2 touchscreen. Tap File Transfer (MTP) or Transfer files.
    Tip: If no popup appears, swipe down from the very top edge of the RC 2 screen to open Android notifications, tap "Charging this device via USB", and select "File Transfer".
  4. Verify in Windows Explorer: Open Windows File Explorer (Win + E). You should see DJI RC 2 listed under "This PC" / "Portable Devices", confirming access to the internal storage path:
    /Android/data/dji.go.v5/files/waypoint
  5. 1-Click Mission Transfer: When recognized, the sidebar displays `🟢 RC 2 USB Link: Connected`. Click "⚡ Send to RC 2" to transfer your active KMZ mission and route thumbnail directly!
DJI Fly Cache Refresh: If an updated mission doesn't immediately show up on the RC 2 screen, simply tap back to the Fly app home screen and reopen the Route library.
📱 Android / Samsung Tablet Field Transfer: Plan flights in Chrome on your Samsung phone or tablet and transfer missions to your DJI RC 2 in the field without any PC!
⚡ Method 1: Zero-Install (Samsung "My Files" via USB-C Cable) — Works Today!
  1. Connect USB-C to USB-C: Plug a USB-C data cable between your Samsung tablet/phone and the DJI RC 2 controller.
  2. Select "File Transfer (MTP)" on the RC 2: When prompted on the RC 2 touchscreen, tap File Transfer. (If no prompt appears, swipe down from the top edge and select USB notification).
  3. Export KMZ from Aalaapi Sky: In your browser on the tablet, tap Export KMZ in the sidebar to download your flight plan to your device's Downloads folder.
  4. Copy via Samsung "My Files" App:
    • Open the built-in My Files app on your Samsung tablet.
    • Go to Internal storage > Download, long-press your exported .kmz file and tap Copy.
    • Under Storage, tap DJI RC 2 (or USB Storage), and navigate to:
      Internal shared storage/Android/data/dji.go.v5/files/waypoint/[UUID]/
    • Tap Copy here. Launch DJI Fly on the RC 2 and your mission is ready!
🛠️ Method 2: Running Companion Service on Android (Termux + ADB)
  1. Install Termux: Install Termux on your Android device (from F-Droid or GitHub).
  2. Install Node.js & ADB Tools: Run the following command in Termux:
    pkg update && pkg install nodejs git android-tools
  3. Start the Companion Bridge: Inside the Aalaapi-Sky directory in Termux, run:
    npm run companion
  4. 1-Click Sync in Tablet Browser: Open Chrome on your tablet, navigate to Aalaapi Sky, and the companion box will illuminate `🟢 Bridge Service: Online` with ADB 1-click sync enabled!
📂 Folder Watcher Method: Watches your PC's Downloads folder. Whenever you click Export KMZ, the watcher detects the files and automatically moves them to the connected RC 2 controller.
  1. Start the Watcher Script: Double-click rc2-sync.bat in the repository root (or tools\rc2-sync.bat). A PowerShell watcher window will appear.
  2. Connect Your RC 2 Controller: Plug in your DJI RC 2 via USB-C in File Transfer mode. The watcher script will confirm the detected controller storage.
  3. Click "Export KMZ" in Aalaapi Sky: Export your mission normally. The script detects <UUID>.kmz and <UUID>.jpg in your Downloads folder and instantly transfers both into the controller's active waypoint directory.
System Compatibility: This tool compiles missions into the modern DJI WPML V2 format.
  • Supported Drones: DJI Mini 4 Pro, DJI Air 3, Mavic 3 Series (Classic/Pro/Enterprise), Inspire 3, Matrice 30/300/350, and other newer aircraft using the DJI Fly App or DJI Pilot 2.
  • Unsupported Drones: Older DJI models (e.g., DJI Mini 3 Pro, Mavic Air 2, Mavic 2, Phantom 4, etc.) which do not support WPML V2 waypoint files.

Since the consumer DJI Fly App does not support direct KML/KMZ waypoint imports from its user interface, you can use a directory overwrite workaround to load this custom flight mission manually:

  1. Create placeholder mission(s): On your DJI RC2 controller, open the DJI Fly App, select Waypoint Flight, and create a short dummy mission (e.g., place 2 arbitrary waypoints) for each part of your flight. Name them memorably (e.g., Placeholder Part 1, Placeholder Part 2) and Save them.
  2. Connect your RC2 to your PC or Android Phone: Use a data-capable USB-C cable to connect your RC2 controller to your computer or an Android phone (like a Samsung Galaxy with MTP support).
  3. Locate the Waypoints Directory: In your computer's file explorer or your phone's file manager app, navigate to the controller's internal storage: 
    DJI RC 2/Internal Shared Storage/Android/data/dji.go.v5/files/waypoint/
    Note: On Android phones, you can use a file manager app (like Samsung "My Files" or "Files by Google") to browse the RC2 storage. On macOS, you will need to use OpenMTP or a similar utility.
  4. Identify your dummy missions: Inside that directory, you will see folders with random names. Look for the most recently updated folders containing a .kmz file.
  5. Inject the generated files:
    • For a single mission: Rename the downloaded `.kmz` file to match the exact filename of the dummy `.kmz` (e.g., Waypoint_XXX.kmz), and copy it into that folder, overwriting the original.
    • For a split mission: Take each extracted part KMZ file (e.g., Part1.kmz) and overwrite the dummy KMZ in its respective placeholder folder.
  6. Fly the mission: Unplug the controller, open the DJI Fly App, go to your saved waypoint missions, and load the placeholders. You will see your newly generated grid patterns ready on the screen!
Safety Warning: Always verify your path on the RC2 screen prior to takeoff. Make sure the relative waypoint heights do not intersect with any trees, buildings, or powerlines in your actual flight environment. Maintain visual line of sight during automated flights!

About Aalaapi Sky

Aalaapi Sky

Version 1.88.0
Designed for DJI Fly, Mini 4 Pro, Air 3, and Enterprise Waypoint Operations

A browser-based procedural waypoint flight mission generator for DJI drones. Converts mission paths to DJI-compliant WPML KMZ archives with full template, camera, gimbal, and speed action tags.

Key Capabilities

  • Standalone, client-side application running 100% in your browser.
  • Full support for DJI Mini 4 Pro, Air 3, Mavic 3, Matrice 30, and Matrice 300/350 RTK.
  • Live NOAA aviation weather & flight category (VFR/MVFR/IFR) verification.
  • Direct RC 2 USB Sync: Export missions and pull flight logs directly to your controller over USB.
  • Preserve imported filename and structure on KMZ exports.
  • Procedural flight generation: 2D Nadir Grid, 3D Double Grid, Oblique Orbits, and Multi-Tier Hybrid passes.
  • Multi-Layer Missions: Combine multiple independent pattern layers with customizable inter-layer transitions.
  • 3D Exclusion Zones & Automated Detour Routing: Define restricted no-fly volumes with 3D altitude bounds and automatic obstacle avoidance routing (Perimeter, Over-the-Top Climb, or Smart 3D).
  • Interactive Gimbal Pitch Visualizer & Upward Tilt: Full $-90^\circ$ to $+60^\circ$ tilt range support with live FOV cone diagram and angle guides.
  • Controlled Settings Reset & Storage Manager: Selectively manage and restore saved browser settings with minimalist indicator dots on modified controls.
  • Global Max Flight Ceiling & Safe RTH Altitude: Enforce legal altitude caps (120m/400ft) and configure safe obstacle-clearing return heights.
